Priorities

Top 3 fixable issues (do these first)

1. Own everyday buyer wording

Publish plain pages (and sections in /llms.txt) that say when Checkly is and is not the right pick versus simple uptime tools. AI copies the internet’s phrasing — “synthetics-only” language misses “uptime” searches.

2. Ship comparison pages you control

Checkly vs Datadog Synthetics, vs Better Stack, vs Hyperping, vs UptimeRobot — honest, dated, with pricing posture. “X vs Y” prompts are high intent.

3. Earn mentions where answers already come from

For uptime-like topics, Aug 2026 Glotier measurements pointed at Reddit, Quora, YouTube, and vendor docs more than G2 for some engines. A perfect site with zero community proof still loses the shortlist.
