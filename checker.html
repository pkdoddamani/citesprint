What each result means

  • Early First two or three tools named. You're on the shortlist buyers actually read.
  • Mid Named, but after the leaders. Buyers may stop reading before they reach you.
  • Late A passing mention near the end or in an "also consider" line. Close to invisible.
  • Not named You're missing from that answer. If this happens on prompts 1–3 but prompt 4 describes you well, assistants know you exist but don't recommend you: usually a content gap (comparison pages, FAQ answers, consistent positioning), not an awareness gap.

One run per prompt is a snapshot, not a rate: answers change between runs, accounts and days. Treat a pattern across several prompts as the signal, not any single cell.
